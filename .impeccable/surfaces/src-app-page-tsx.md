# Surface: TGF site (all routes share one premium system)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: farm customers, book readers, tech employers/clients, Sip & Shelf/Aegis partners, equally. Job: understand TGF in seconds, open the venture you came for, act on it. Constraints: static export, no backend; 5+ years everywhere; current employer hidden; book links per PRODUCT.md; original content kept in full.

Build path: code-led (no image generation in this environment).

Information architecture: global nav (Farming, Books, IT-DevOps, Perfume, About, Contact). Each venture is its own section of pages with a sticky local tab bar: Farming (Overview, Dairy, Harvest, Gallery, Order), Books (Overview, Author), IT-DevOps (Overview, Products, Experience, Projects, Skills). Home is a hub of venture tiles, not a long single page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: TGF presented the way the best premium brands present products: each venture a flagship with its own pages. Refuses the one long page that lists everything.

OWN-WORLD: White and #F5F5F7 sections, near-black #1D1D1F type, one brand green #0B6E4F for links and pill buttons, black tiles for IT-DevOps and Perfume. Inter at tight tracking, 17px body. Large rounded tiles (28px), full-bleed real photography, no borders or decoration.

STORY: A visitor sees a serious, well-made company, opens their venture from the home tiles or the nav, moves through its tabs, and acts: order, buy, book a call, see the work, follow the launch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred headline "Rooted in Punjab. Built for today." with a one-line subhead and two actions, the real farm hero photo below it in a wide rounded frame; the next tile (Farming) starts in view on desktop.

FORM: The premium brand standard (canon), chosen by the user after rejecting the assigned and pick directions; seed:4688db93263cf66f. Signature interaction: the sticky local tab bar on every venture page, with the current tab marked and a primary action at its right.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
